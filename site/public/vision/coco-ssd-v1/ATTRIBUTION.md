TensorFlow.js COCO-SSD (Google / TensorFlow authors)

Source: https://github.com/tensorflow/tfjs-models/tree/master/coco-ssd
Model weights: https://storage.googleapis.com/tfjs-models/savedmodel/ssdlite_mobilenet_v2/model.json
License: Apache License 2.0; original LICENSE included.

Unmodified model graph and five weight shards, fetched over verified TLS.
Per-file SHA-256 checksums and upstream URLs are in manifest.json.
Recognizes 80 COCO object classes. No face identity, speech, activity recognition,
emotion inference, story understanding, or engagement prediction is provided.
Browser-only CPU worker inference; no video frames are sent to model vendors.
